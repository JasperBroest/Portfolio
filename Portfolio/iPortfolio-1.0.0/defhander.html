Defhander

  • Category: Mixed Reality
  • Project date: 07 November, 2024
  • Time: 4 weeks
  • Made with: Unity, C#, MetaXR SDK

Play a tower defense in your house!

This is a mixed reality tower defense game. You can place towers on the playfield and buy upgrades from the shop to help defeat the monsters that are trying to invade the castle.

You can push or throw enemies of the map. Or you can block them with your hand. You can even pick them up. And if you save up enough money you can even pickup the map itself.

This projects was fun to make with a lot of different challenges. I mostly worked on setting up mixed reality in unity and making everything work with MR throughout the project. I also worked on the enemies and some buttons and helped with game logic.

Adjust to your likings

Adjust the position of the playfield and the shop to your likings!

Use turrets to defend

Place turrets anywhere on the field to defend against incoming enemies! You can buy them in the shop.

Interact with the enemies

You can push or throw enemies off the map, block them with your hands, or even pick them up!

Clean the board!

You can even clean the board by flipping it!
