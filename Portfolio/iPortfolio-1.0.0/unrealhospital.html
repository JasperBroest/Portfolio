Dont panic!

This is my first project in Unreal Engine. Its a VR experience where you play as a stressed nurse in a hospital. You have to help patients before their time runs out. Your goal is to make it to the end of your shift without losing any patients.
Or going insane...

Help Patients

Use bandages to heal patients. Just grab a bandage and touch the patient's wound with it.

Hurry!

Dont let the patients wait too long or they will die! And you will start to go insane...

Dont get overwhelmed!

The longer your shift goes on the more patients will arrive. You have to keep up!
