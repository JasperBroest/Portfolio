VR Tour Utrecht

  • Category: 360 Video
  • Project date: 19 April, 2024
  • Time: 4 weeks
  • Made with: Unity, C#, MetaXR SDK, 360° camera

Imagine you can get a tour through Utrecht at home.

Well that is possible with this 360 degrees tour. Me and a fellow student went through Utrecht(city in the Netherlands) and filmed all the content for this VR tour ourselfs. Then we put all the content in unity and made you able to look around like you're in the place itself. I find this a fun and really unique project to look back on.
